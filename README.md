Junk Removal Cost Estimator (Kent, WA)

A simple, open-source calculator that helps homeowners in the Kent, WA area get a rough idea of junk removal costs before booking a service — based on item type, estimated volume, and access difficulty.

Why This Exists

Most junk removal pricing is quote-based, which is fair but makes it hard to compare options quickly. This tool gives a ballpark estimate using publicly available regional pricing patterns, so you can sanity-check any quote you receive.

Features


Estimate cost by item category (furniture, appliances, mattresses, yard waste, construction debris)
Adjust for access difficulty (ground floor vs. stairs vs. no elevator)
Rough volume-based pricing tiers (single item, quarter load, half load, full load)
No sign-up, no data collection — runs entirely client-side



Disclaimer

This is a rough estimate tool only. Actual pricing varies by company, current disposal fees, and specific job conditions. For an accurate quote in the Kent, WA and King County area, a local junk removal company can provide a free on-site or photo-based estimate.

If your estimate involves bulky or unusual items, it's worth checking a full list of accepted items before assuming something can't be picked up.
